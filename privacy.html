3D Anatomy

Privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026. 3D Anatomy is operated from Islamabad, Pakistan. Contact: officercon9@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. 3D Anatomy has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To work the way you left it, the app saves your settings and study progress in your device's local storage: language, body model choice, low-power setting, bookmarks, recently viewed structures, lesson progress, flashcard schedules, quiz results and activity counts. This information never leaves your device. Clearing the app's storage, using Clear data in the app's system settings, or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Network use

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address) under GitHub's own privacy statement. The Android app contains its content and does not need an internet connection. Fonts are included in the app and are not loaded from third parties.

Permissions

The Android app asks for no special permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Health information

Please do not type patient or personal health information anywhere in the app. It is not designed to store it.

Children

The app is meant for students and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and contact

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For any privacy question write to officercon9@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.
